# Bleach

## About the series

**Bleach** follows *Ichigo Kurosaki*, a teenager who gains the powers of a Soul Reaper (Shinigami). He protects the living world from evil spirits called Hollows and helps guide souls to the afterlife.

> My favorite arc is **Hueco Mundo**.

## Why I like the Hueco Mundo arc

Aizen's Arrancar take Orihime, and Ichigo and his friends travel to Hueco Mundo to save her.

- The rescue mission feels exciting and personal
- Las Noches is a memorable place
- The Espada are strong enemies with different fighting styles
- Ichigo's fights with Grimmjow and Ulquiorra
- Ichigo's Hollow powers become an important part of the story
